📦 pim - Share files between your devices

Download for Windows

pim helps you move files, text, notes, and tasks between your computers and phones. All transfers happen over your home or office Wi-Fi network. Your data stays on your local devices and does not go to the internet.

🚀 How to get started

Follow these steps to set up pim on your Windows computer.

  1. Visit the download page to find the installer.
  2. Look for the file ending in .exe for Windows.
  3. Click the file to start the download.
  4. Open the file once the download finishes to begin the installation.
  5. Follow the prompts on your screen to complete the setup.
  6. Launch the pim application from your desktop or start menu.

💻 System requirements

Ensure your computer meets these requirements for the best experience:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • A stable Wi-Fi connection.
  • At least 100 MB of space on your drive.
  • Both devices must connect to the same local network.

    • 📂 How to send files

    Once you open the app, you will see a simple list of your other devices on the network.

    1. Select the device you want to share with from the list.
    2. Drag and drop your file into the app window.
    3. Alternatively, click the plus icon to browse for files.
    4. Click send. The file moves to the other device instantly over your Wi-Fi.

    📝 Sharing text and notes

    You can send short notes or clipboard text just like files.

    • Paste your text into the input field.
    • Pick your recipient device.
    • Click the share button.
    • The text appears on the other screen immediately.

      • ✅ Frequently asked questions

      Do I need an account? No. You do not need a login or password to use this tool.

      Is this safe? Yes. Data stays within your local network. It does not touch cloud servers.

      Can I use this on Android? Yes. Download the version for Android to sync with your phone.
